# AI Parlay Builder Short Recorded Demo Script

## Goal

Record a calm 3 to 4 minute demo that explains the product clearly without needing a live sales call.

This script is designed to help the product sell itself.

## Before recording

1. launch the app locally
2. open `View Preferences`
3. click `Reset To Buyer Demo Defaults`
4. confirm:
   - `NBA`
   - `Sportsbook`
   - `Core`
   - `Simple`
   - `Light`

Use demo-backed workflows if live data looks noisy.

## Opening line

`AI Parlay Builder is a workflow and intelligence platform for prop research, ticket construction, DFS handoff, and post-result learning.`

`The goal is to help an operator scan markets, build tickets, review outcomes, and improve future decisions inside one product.`

## Part 1: Overview

Stay here for about 45 to 60 seconds.

Say:

`The app opens in a calmer guided view. Instead of dropping the operator into a raw board, Overview gives them operating posture, workflow guidance, and the next best actions.`

Point to:

- `Today's Operating Mode`
- `Workflow Snapshot`
- `Suggested Next Steps`
- `Notification Center`

Say:

`This is important because it makes the product easier to understand, demo, and inherit.`

## Part 2: Edge Scanner

Stay here for about 35 to 45 seconds.

Say:

`Edge Scanner is where the product turns the market board into ranked candidates instead of asking the operator to scan everything manually.`

Point to:

- ranked rows
- filters
- confidence or edge fields

Say:

`This is where the intelligence layer starts becoming useful in practice.`

## Part 3: Parlay Lab

Stay here for about 45 to 60 seconds.

Say:

`Parlay Lab takes those ranked candidates and turns them into a more structured build workflow for sportsbook or DFS use cases.`

Point to:

- profile or build controls
- recommended structure
- stake planning
- slip or build output

Say:

`This makes the app more than a dashboard. It becomes a usable workflow product.`

## Part 4: Results & Grading

Stay here for about 45 to 60 seconds.

Say:

`Results and Grading is where the product keeps memory. Saved tickets, tracked picks, and review workflow make it possible to look back at what happened and improve future decisions.`

Point to:

- workflow status
- saved tickets
- tracked picks
- bankroll journal or review area

Say:

`That is what helps the product move from one-time use into repeat operational value.`

## Part 5: Optional Pro mention

Stay here for 15 to 20 seconds only if needed.

Say:

`The app also has a deeper Pro layer for audits, review, and backtesting, but the product can be demoed in a calmer surface first so it does not overwhelm a new buyer or operator.`

Do not linger here.

## Closing line

`AI Parlay Builder combines scan, build, track, and learn inside one workflow product. It can be demonstrated calmly, reviewed deeply, and handed off with much less guesswork than a typical internal-only tool.`

## Recording tips

- keep the recording under 4 minutes
- do not explain every tab
- do not spend time on provider keys
- do not force live data if demo-backed mode is cleaner
- keep the cursor movement slow and intentional
