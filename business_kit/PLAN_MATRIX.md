# AI Parlay Builder Plan Matrix

## Purpose

This matrix is for packaging conversations, buyer handoff, and future monetization framing.

The plans below describe product posture, not a public billing system that is already live.

## Quick positioning

- `Core` = calmer first-run workflow
- `Pro` = deeper analyst and review surface
- `Owner` = operator and handoff layer

## Plan comparison

| Plan | Best for | Main value | Included surfaces |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Core` | buyers, demos, first-run users | shows the product clearly without surfacing every diagnostic at once | Overview guidance, Live Board, Edge Scanner, Parlay Lab, saved tickets, grading, bankroll journal |
| `Pro` | active operators, analysts, tuners | opens the smarter review and audit depth that makes the workflow more explainable | everything in `Core` plus smart score audit, parlay leg audit, learning tables, source review, weekly/monthly review, backtest, CLV diagnostics |
| `Owner` | buyer handoff, admin control, product operations | adds packaging and transfer readiness so the product is easier to operate and inherit | everything in `Pro` plus packaging controls, default-view controls, sync policy controls, owner summary export, handoff docs bundle |

## Included by plan

### Core

Use when you want the cleanest product story.

Included:

- overview operating guidance
- live board inspection
- edge scanner
- parlay lab
- saved tickets and grading workflow
- bankroll journal

What it does well:

- keeps the app approachable
- gives a buyer or first-run operator a calmer experience
- supports demos without forcing every advanced surface into view

### Pro

Use when you want the full analyst story.

Included:

- everything in `Core`
- smart score audit
- parlay leg audit
- smart pick learning tables
- source performance and experiment logs
- weekly and monthly review diagnostics
- backtest and CLV diagnostics

What it does well:

- makes the intelligence layer more explainable
- supports tuning and deeper review
- increases trust for a technical operator

### Owner

Use when you want the product to be easier to package, transfer, and operate.

Included:

- everything in `Pro`
- plan packaging controls
- default-view and feature-surface controls
- sync policy and local operating controls
- buyer-handoff-friendly admin summary
- downloadable handoff docs bundle

What it does well:

- reduces handoff friction
- makes the product easier to inherit
- gives a future buyer a clearer operator package

## Recommended posture by audience

### If you are demoing the product

Use:

- `Core`
- `Simple` view

Why:

- it makes the product easier to understand quickly

### If you are actively tuning the model/workflow

Use:

- `Pro`
- `Pro` view

Why:

- it opens the deeper review surfaces without changing the core workflow

### If you are preparing for handoff or sale

Use:

- `Owner`
- `Simple` view first, then `Pro` view if needed

Why:

- it gives the cleanest operational posture while keeping transfer assets visible

## Cleanest buyer explanation

If a buyer asks what the plans mean, the simplest explanation is:

`Core is the clean product surface, Pro is the deeper analyst layer, and Owner is the operating and handoff layer.`
