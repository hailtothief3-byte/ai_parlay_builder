# AI Parlay Builder Listing Description

## Short version

AI Parlay Builder is a workflow and intelligence platform for prop research, ticket construction, DFS handoff, and post-result learning.

It combines live and demo board review, smart ranking, build workflows, saved-ticket memory, results review, and bankroll tracking inside one product surface.

The app is already packaged with a calmer demo mode, a deeper review layer, and buyer-handoff documentation, which makes it easier to evaluate and transfer than a typical internal-only tool.

## Marketplace-style version

AI Parlay Builder is a working sports workflow product built around four jobs: scan, build, track, and learn.

The product helps an operator review live or demo-backed prop boards, rank opportunities, build sportsbook or DFS-style tickets, save and review those tickets later, and use graded history to improve future decision-making.

What makes the asset attractive is that it is more than a dashboard. It already includes a guided overview layer, ranked edge scanning, structured parlay-building workflows, ticket memory, bankroll tracking, and explainable review surfaces. It also includes packaging controls and buyer-handoff documents that reduce transfer friction.

This asset is likely strongest for a solo operator, a small sports workflow studio, or a niche productivity-tool buyer who wants an existing product surface rather than a raw experimental codebase.

The product is not positioned as an automated wager-placement tool.

## Ultra-short outreach version

AI Parlay Builder is a workflow and intelligence product for prop research, ticket building, DFS handoff, and post-result learning. It is already live, demoable, and packaged with handoff documentation for transfer.
